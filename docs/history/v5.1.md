# v5.1 (Nov 13, 2026)

## 1. Any PoB imports now, not just ones with acts in the names
- **Why your second PoB failed:** its trees were named only "60", "94" and "22", and its one skill set had no name at all. The importer only understood act names (and numbers written after "Level"/"Lvl"), so every stage came out empty. It still said "Imported" but showed "no skill set / No passive tree".
- **What it does now** depends on the names in the PoB:
  - **Act names** (Act 1, Act 3-10, …) work as before: one tab per act plus Early, Mid and Late maps, and your existing PoB comes out exactly the same.
  - **Level names** ("22", "Level 22", "Lvl 22", "lv22", "L22") give one tab per level, sorted by level. The tracker shows the highest level that is at or below your character's level (Char level in Defense), and switches by itself as you level. Click any tab to look at another one; **back to level N** returns to the automatic choice.
  - **Other names** become tabs you switch by hand. "Early Mapping", "Red Maps" and "End Game" still go to Early, Mid or Late maps where the name makes that clear. The tab you pick is remembered.
  - **One tree and one skill set** (or no names at all) means no tabs: you get one gem layout and one tree for the whole build.
  - **Mixed PoBs** work too. Act names go on acts, map names go on map stages, anything else becomes its own tab. "Early Game" counts as Act 1.
- Skill sets and trees are paired by name. When the counts don't match, a stage without its own skill set or tree uses the one before it (or the untitled one), so no tab is ever empty.
- If a PoB uses a tree version the tracker doesn't ship (3_27, 3_28), the tree is drawn on the nearest one it does have. The caption says so.
- The act timer, pace, Next Up and everything else work the same whatever the PoB looks like.
- **Voice:** "next tree", "previous tree", "show tree Level 60", "show the tree for red maps", "back to current tree".

## 2. Wiki links are links, not clipboard copies
- "Get wiki link for X" no longer touches your clipboard. The page shows up in the **Wiki links** panel as a link you can click when you're ready; it opens in a new tab.
- The spoken answer is now "Wiki link for X added."
- If you want a link on the clipboard, each row still has a **Copy** button. It only copies when you click it.

## 3. Edit stage mapping and Maps stage now visibly work
- **Edit stage mapping** did open, but at the very bottom of the import box, below the long import summary and off screen. So clicking it looked like nothing happened.
  - It now opens right under the Links & tree header and scrolls into view, with your current stage highlighted.
  - It has a **Done** button. Each change shows at once (cards and tree) and is saved.
  - There's a second **Edit stage mapping** button next to Import PoB build.
  - The import summary is now folded under "What went where".
- **Maps stage (Early/Mid/Late)** only mattered once you were in maps, so changing it during the campaign did nothing you could see.
  - It's now a row of buttons: **Auto / Early / Mid / Late**. Clicking one shows that stage right away, the button stays lit, and a "pinned" badge appears.
  - In maps, the pinned stage is "now". **Auto** goes back to following your map tier.

## 4. Readable links
- Every link in the app is now light sky blue (#8cc8ff) and semi-bold. Links you've visited turn soft lilac (#cdb4ff), and links underline on hover. This covers history, research answers, the Fate panel, Wiki links, PoB help and character help.
- Contrast against the grey panels: 9.8:1 on the panel, 9.0:1 on the lighter panel and 10.8:1 on the page background. The visited colour is 8.8–10.6:1. The accessibility minimum is 4.5:1, and the old dark blue was about 2:1.

## 5. Screenshot (F10 / paste) reads your character level
- The Character panel doesn't write the word "Level". The level is the number in the round badge at the bottom-left of the portrait, so the reader used to skip it.
- It now finds the panel header (the Life and Mana labels, with your character name and area line just above them) and reads the number in the badge below the portrait, to the left of Life/Mana.
  - A single digit like "4" is easy for OCR to miss. When that happens, it reads just the badge area again, enlarged, and only accepts a number several reads agree on (usually 0.1–0.2 s extra).
  - Only 1–100 counts. With no character header on screen, no level is guessed.
- The level fills **Char level** in Defense after you confirm, and a level-based PoB then switches to the matching tab.
- A screenshot of just the header (level only) is offered as a normal "confirm" instead of "incomplete".
- A resistance read as "60% (-60%)" (minus sign dropped) is now taken as -60.
