# v3 (Nov 12, 2026), from the author's v2 session feedback

## 1. Links per stage, by category, from your own PoB
- `voice/pob_import.py`: takes a PoB export code, a pobb.in or poe.ninja/pob link, or XML (tracker: Links & tree → Import; console: `companion.py --import-pob …`).
- Stage mapping:
  - Act 1…Act 10 come from sets/trees titled "Act N"; "Act 4-10" covers a range, each act keeping its own tree.
  - Early/Mid/Late maps come from the map sets, paired with trees by the `{n}` tag, then by title similarity and level. Several variants per stage, the first is the default.
  - Colour codes (`^6`, `^xRRGGBB`) are stripped everywhere.
  - Anything left unmatched is listed and can be assigned by hand.
- Categories:
  - The seven categories are main attack, movement, utility, auras, curse, minions, other.
  - They come from your socket-group labels and `==== Main Skills / Utility / Auras / Support Spectres ====` headers, else from the gems.
  - Override any group in edit mode. The config is stored in the browser (`poe330_build_cfg_v3`) and exportable as JSON.
- Your PoB (Witch Necromancer, 17 sets, 23 trees) is the embedded default and replaces the Ziz build_plan.
- Voice:
  - "act 1 main attack", "what's my movement", "utility links in act 5", "what are my links", "early maps main attack", "what are my spectres".
  - Answers follow PoB order with your notes: "Rolling Magma with Arcane Surge and Added Lightning Damage until you get SRS, then Summon Raging Spirit with Added Lightning Damage and Minion Damage".

## 2. Right-side panel
- 4th column, Maxroll-like cards:
  - each card shows the category colour, gem colour circles (r/g/b/w from PoB's gem data), supports indented, disabled gems struck through, and the link count;
  - your label note is shown in gold.
- Passive tree closeup (SVG from PoB's tree data):
  - new nodes vs the previous stage in green, already allocated in gold;
  - class start and path lines (arcs on orbits);
  - auto-cropped to the new nodes, with a mini-map, new notables named in the caption.
- The tree is embedded for 3_29. Other versions are served by the companion from `voice/pobdata`.

## 3. Act mix-up
- Root cause:
  - The current act is the first act whose milestone isn't done.
  - A milestone could be set by loose phrases ("heading to act 3", "starting act 3") or by a carried-over session.
  - There was no way to say which act you're really in: "I'm in Act 2, not Act 3" became a note.
- Fixes:
  - Milestones only on clear completion phrases. "Not / almost / about to / going to / before / until … act N" never marks anything; "heading/moving to act N" is a note.
  - Act bosses count: "Malachai down", "Merveil is dead".
  - New: "I'm in Act N (not Act M)", "set act to N". These unmark later milestones (or mark earlier ones), read back what changed, and "undo" restores them.
  - Rolling log `voice/logs/companion_YYYYMMDD.log`: heard text, actions, readback and timings, plus OCR results and errors, TTS errors, imports and crashes. Kept 14 days.

## 5. OCR
- Any Windows OCR error (winrt 3.2.1 "Invalid parameter count" included) now falls back to rapidocr. The reason is recorded and logged.
- The WinRT bitmap is built from a PNG stream first, then the 4-arg and with-alpha buffer copies.
- rapidocr-onnxruntime is a real requirement. Its angle classifier is turned off, because it flipped "9% (9%)" into "(%6) %6".
- Images under 1500 px are upscaled 2.5× for Windows and 2× for rapidocr.
- Only the left 40% of a full-screen grab or paste is read.
- New fields: mana, armour, evasion, attack block.
- Your Defences screenshot reads life 93, mana 128, armour 31, evasion 40, fire 0, cold 9, lightning 9, chaos 0, attack block 20. That takes about 0.8–1.2 s warm with rapidocr on the box.
- Deploy fix (first Windows install): winrt 3.2.1 also needs `winrt-Windows.Foundation.Collections` (added to requirements). Windows OCR then runs (~240 ms) but drops the coloured res numbers, so `auto` redoes a read with label-but-no-number in rapidocr.
- New: `--ocr-selftest [windows|rapidocr]`, `--ocr-file shot.png --ocr-engine windows`.

## 6. Pool colour
- Green when the pool is above the target, amber at ≥ 75 % of it, red below. The target is shown next to the value.
- Targets: 300 × act in the campaign, 3000 at map start, 5000 from T6.

## 7. Fate bosses (replaces the Fates checklist)
- Shows the bosses of the current act, with the Wickerman reward per viscera tier and the slot.
- Data sources, combined in `voice/fates.py`:
  - boss → viscera from poewiki "Viscera";
  - mod values from poedb "Wickerman";
  - Fate card text from poewiki "Fate".
- The cache refreshes every 6 h in the background, and the tracker embeds a snapshot.
- "Currently unknown" wherever the data has nothing.
- Interactions:
  - click a boss to expand it;
  - search any boss;
  - "killed with 0/1/2/3 Fates" buttons;
  - voice: "fate reward for merveil", "fates for malachai".

## Log fixes
- Questions never log drops. "Where does the cane of kulimac drop" was logging Cane of Unravelling through the short "the cane" alias; a short alias now loses to a longer high-confidence name.
- Drop-where questions answer "say research X". "Which boss drops X" and "who drops X" work too.
- Research:
  - filler stripping ("look up the wiki for X", "on the wiki please");
  - "gym" → gem;
  - several candidate titles, so variant / List of / patch pages lose;
  - full-page sections (Item acquisition, monster/area restrictions);
  - poedb as a second source;
  - the Vendor recipe system page for recipe questions ("how do I make a whetstone" included).
- Fixed a bug where version-history rows ("3.10.0 Introduced") were taken for the table of contents and hid every section.
- "News on X" says research can't read X/Twitter and checks only poewiki and poedb.
- Follow-up res phrasing: "18 lightning as well", "and 20 fire", plurals ("my lightnings are 40").
- "Lunaris and Solaris down" no longer logs a fuzzy "Vix Lunaris" drop.
