# v5.2 changes

## Power Chase items link to their wiki page
- When you add a chase item (typing it, saying it, or loading a template), the tracker quietly looks it up on poewiki.
  If it finds a page it's sure about, the item name turns into a link. The link opens in a new tab.
- It uses the same strict name check as "get wiki link". If poewiki has no page, it tries poedb.
- Adding the item is still instant. The lookup happens a moment later.
- Entries that describe a kind of item rather than one item stay plain text. Examples: "Abyss jewel", "+1/+1 wand",
  "Dread boss fight", "+1 Spectre chest". Names starting with + or a number, and names containing words like jewel,
  boss, chest, wand, ring, helmet, link or craft, count as this kind of entry.
- Examples: "Darkness Enthroned" links to its poewiki page, and "Frostbearers" links to the Forged Frostbearer
  monster page.
- If the companion or the wiki is offline, nothing changes and the tracker tries again later (about once a minute).
  Items you already had are looked up once after the upgrade.
- Results are remembered, so the same name is never looked up twice.
- To change a link, click ✎ on the item. Paste your own link, or clear the box to leave the name unlinked.
  "auto link" goes back to the automatic lookup.

## Configure page (⚙ Configure, next to Panels)
This is a full page, not a pop-up. Close it with ✕ Close or Esc. It has five sections.

### Defense stats
- Choose which stats the Defense checkpoint panel shows and checks.
- The built-in stats can be turned off: life, ES, mana, pool, ele res, overcap, chaos, attack block and spell block.
- New optional stats:
  - Mind Over Matter
  - Evasion rating
  - Evade chance (use it for dodge too)
  - Spell suppression
  - Armour
  - Physical damage reduction
  - Endurance charges
  - Physical damage taken as fire / cold / lightning / chaos
  - Leech per second, with an Overleech tick box
- Each stat you turn on gets a box in the panel.
- **Mind Over Matter:** 40% of damage comes out of mana first. The effective pool is ES + the smaller of life ÷ 0.6
  and life + mana.
  - If you have enough mana (at least ⅔ of your life), you can take life ÷ 0.6 damage.
  - Otherwise mana runs out first and you get life + mana.
  - The panel shows the math, and tier checks and the pool colour use the effective pool.
- **Pool colour:** green above 300 × act in the campaign, 3000 at map start and 5000 from T6. All three numbers can be
  changed here.
- **Screenshot reader (F10):** it now also reads evade chance, spell suppression and physical damage reduction.

### Tier checks
- Set the requirements for six tiers: Map entry, T6+, T11+, T15+, Pinnacle boss ready and Uber ready.
- Requirements can be a minimum number, "capped" for res, or "must be on" for MoM and overleech. Leave a box empty
  to skip that check.
- You're cleared for a tier when it and every tier before it pass. The panel's "Cleared for …" line and its list of
  what's missing per tier come from here.
- Each character has its own tier settings.
  - **Copy from** takes the settings from an archived character or another build's defaults.
  - **Reset to defaults** goes back to the defaults.
  - Settings you haven't touched follow the build if you change it.
- Fate caps per tier can be edited.

### Drop & event triggers
- Every type is listed with its spoken phrases and what it does: logs a drop or event, which Open Trigger Action it
  fires, any tracker change (a death marks the character dead) and the rules it feeds.
- You can add types, rename them, change drop/event, edit phrases, turn them on or off, and delete them.
- **Extra column:** "alert" pops an alert when the type is logged, and "also say" adds a spoken line.
- Built-in events (★) can be turned off but not deleted. A turned-off built-in is logged as a plain note.
- Reset to defaults is available.

### Rules
- Rules count how often something happens in a time window.
- **Built-in rule:** 2 near-deaths within 30 minutes, with repeats within 90 seconds counted as one incident. It puts
  "Stop for the night (S03)" at the top of Next Up and says "END THE SESSION". This works exactly as before.
- **Editable:** the count, the event, the window (0 = this session), the merge time, the wording, and whether it
  speaks, alerts or adds a Next Up task.
- **Add rule** makes your own rule. Built-in rules can be turned off or reset, but not deleted.

### General
- Pace target, text size, panels (show/hide, reset layout), content types and colours.
- Companion status, plus the chase wiki options: on/off, look up now, re-check all.
- The quick controls stay in the header.

## Default tier checks (a starting point)

| Tier | Res | Overcap | Chaos | Pool (relaxed bar / standard & melee) | Spell block (spell-block builds) | Fate cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Map entry (T1–5) | capped | – | ≥ 0 | 3000 / 3200 | – | 1 |
| T6+ (T6–10) | capped | yes | ≥ 30 | 5000 / 5000 | – | 2 |
| T11+ (T11–14) | capped | yes | ≥ 60 | 5000 / 6000 | ≥ 50 | 3 |
| T15+ | capped | yes | ≥ 75 | 5500 / 7000 | ≥ 50 | 3 |
| Pinnacle boss ready | capped | yes | ≥ 75 | 6500 / 8000 | ≥ 50 | 3 |
| Uber ready | capped | yes | ≥ 75 | 8000 / 10000 | ≥ 50 | 3 |

- Spectre Necromancer uses the relaxed bar. ED Occultist is standard.
- One change from v5.1: the T6+ pool is now 5000 for every bar, to match the "5000 from T6" rule. It was 4000 relaxed
  and 4500 standard.

## Saved data
- Everything is saved in the browser and is part of Download backup / Restore: the tier profile, the rules, the wiki
  lookups and the options.
- v5.1 data upgrades with nothing lost.
