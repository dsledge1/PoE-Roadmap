# v5.3 (Nov 13, 2026): league-launch fixes

Built from an Act 1–2 playtest log. Every phrase heard in that log is now a regression test.
Custom hotkeys in `voice\config.json` (for example the backtick `) keep working.

## Fixes from the log
- **Confirming a screenshot read**: "Confirmed.", "confirmed", "yes confirm", "apply" and "looks good" now apply a pending OCR read (before, only "yes" worked).
- **"Fair Graves is dead"** is a boss kill now. It is no longer read as your death (no P1, no Cane of Unravelling).
- **"1 minutes paused"** now says "1 minute".
- **Unknown boss in a Fate question**: you get "I didn't catch the boss name (heard …)". It no longer says "Campaign: zero Fates".
- **"s for the great white beast"**: the boss name is pulled out correctly now.

## Lab trials (new)
- Tracks the 6 normal-lab trials: Lower Prison (A1), Chamber of Sins (A2), Crypt (A2), Crematorium (A3), Catacombs (A3), Imperial Gardens (A3).
- "Lab trial done" marks the trial for the area you're in, or the next one you haven't done. "Chamber of Sins trial done" marks that one.
- "Missed the lab trial in the prison" marks it **missed**. Next Up then reminds you about missed trials before the lab.
- Corrections: "no, not normal lab", "normal lab was not done" and "that's wrong" unmark Normal lab and anything it marked as skipped.

## Timer: new run (new)
- "Restart timer", "reset timer" and "new run" ask first. Say **yes** within 30 seconds and a fresh run starts.
- The old run is kept in history. Level and band are not touched. Undo works.

## Wiki links
- "pull link for X", "pull the wiki link for X", "link for X", "wiki X" and "research link for X" all open wiki links.
- A trailing "card" is dropped, and divination-card pages are preferred when you say "card". So "the death card" opens Death.
- "wiki what is …" is still a research question.

## Fate reward questions
- "What's the reward for <boss>", "what fate does <boss> give" and "what are the fate boss rewards for <boss>" now answer from the Fate list.
- Boss names are matched loosely, so "Caliph Headstaver" finds Calaf, Headstaver.

## Where you are, and boss kills (Acts 1–3)
- Area chatter like "we're in the Submerged Passage" or "fighting Merveil" sets your current area. It moves the act forward if the area implies it, and the Fate panel opens that area's boss.
- Boss kills such as "Hillock down", "Fairgraves is dead", "Merveil dead" and "Fadeletus" add a boss-kill event to the log. They also record the kill (0 Fates) in the Fate panel.

## Screenshot reading (OCR)
- "00/0" misreads (for example "750/0") now read as a percent.
- Glued labels such as "Versi01" are no longer read as a number.
- The Maximum Energy Shield header value is used only when it's clear. The Defences row still wins.

## EHP on the Defense panel (new, an approximation)
A PoB-style line under the Defense stats, for example:
`Max hit: Phys 3275 · Fire 12.5k · Cold 12.0k · Light 13.0k · Chaos 2500 · EHP ~6825`
It's on by default. Turn it off in ⚙ Configure → Defense stats → EHP. A tier can also require "EHP ≥ N".

How it's worked out (hover the ⓘ for the same text):
- **Pool** = life + ES. With Mind Over Matter, mana is added to the pool, up to the MoM share.
- **Physical**: armour reduction = A ÷ (A + 5 × hit), solved at the hit itself. If armour isn't entered, the in-game phys damage reduction is used instead (it already includes armour). Each endurance charge adds 4% when that stat is on. The total is capped at 90%. "Phys taken as" moves that share to the element and uses that element's resistance.
- **Fire, Cold, Lightning**: max hit = pool ÷ (1 − resistance).
- **Chaos**: max hit = pool ÷ (1 − chaos res). ES is not bypassed.
- **EHP**: an average over a hit mix (each type equally, half attacks and half spells). Attack hits are scaled by (1 − attack block) × (1 − evade chance). Spell hits are scaled by (1 − spell block) × (1 − ½ × suppression).
- It's a guide. Flasks, guard skills, regen, recoup and PoB's exact hit weighting are not modelled.
